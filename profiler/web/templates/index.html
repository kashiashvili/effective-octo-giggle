Find people who think like you

Connect your favourite apps — GitHub, Netflix, Goodreads, Google, Facebook — and discover like-minded people. Your raw data stays on your device; only a privacy-preserving fingerprint is shared.

How it works

1. Connect your sources

Link GitHub, upload your Goodreads or Netflix export, or supply an API token for Google / Facebook.

2. Generate your fingerprint

We extract interest signals and compute a MinHash fingerprint. Your raw data is never stored or transmitted.

3. Meet your matches

We compare fingerprints to surface the users most similar to you — ranked by shared interests.
